# Demo Prompts

Recommended walkthrough:

1. `Give me a quick brief about Cardano today.`
2. `What is ADA? Include tokenomics, risks, and catalysts.`
3. `Compare ADA, BTC, and ETH.`
4. `Show risk signals for ADA.`
5. `What happened this week in the Cardano ecosystem?`
6. `Show me Cardano projects to watch.`

Expected behavior:
- Chat shows companion summary cards per turn.
- Canvas updates with structured crypto blocks.
- Suggestions change based on current context.
